# Phase 0 lexical policy

Generated by `node tools/report_lexicon.js`. Tiers: **curated** = enabled in v1.1; **attested** = a source states it directly; **derived** = all premises attested plus a cited grammatical rule; **inferred** = heuristic premise, recorded but never enabled in the default profile. Readings disabled in v1.1 stay disabled.

## Derived collective nouns (45)

Rule: the first-listed OEWN 2025 noun sense is `noun.group` **and** that sense's hypernym chain reaches *social group*, *animal group*, *gathering (of persons)* or *people* (matched by synset lemma set, not lemma string). Consequence: a plural-agreement reading (CGEL ch. 5; usual in NZ/British English): *the staff are*, *staff sign*, *many staff*. Visible quirks of OEWN sense ordering remain (e.g. *energy*, *song*) and are tagged `derived` so the ERG run and players can see them.

Excluded from usage mining: *to* (infinitives), *of* (mass/genitive definitional use), and uses followed by *of*/*to* (complex prepositions such as *in front of*). Only nouns with no mass reading receive this licence, since mass nouns are bare anyway.

## Gold-tile role policy

A gold helper supplies only closed-class roles (determiner, pronoun, preposition, modal, auxiliary, copula, coordinator, subordinator, complementiser, relativiser, negator, degree adverb), never a noun/verb/adjective reading of the same spelling. Explicit exceptions by user decision: *has* keeps its lexical-verb reading (*has blame*); *for* is both preposition and causal coordinator. Only the 47 v1.1 helper candidates can be gold. Blue tiles keep every supported reading (blue *can* is noun, verb or modal).
