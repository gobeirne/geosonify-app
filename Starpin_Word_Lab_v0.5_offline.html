How to use Word Lab

1. Collect. Use the simulated HEALPix discovery buttons, pick your original 74 addresses, or collect any BIP39 word by name.

2. Unlock. Click the grammatical helper buttons. Gold helper tiles can be used as often as you like. If a word exists in both inventories (for example, can), the blue collectible copy scores points and the gold helper copy doesn't.

3. Build. Tap words to add them, tap the + gaps to set insertion position, drag on desktop, or use arrows to move words. Hypothetical purple words can be tried before they're collected.

4. Inspect. Valid sentences receive a provisional score and a labelled grammar tree. Absurd meaning is fine; grammar must be supported. Long lists and extra adjectives have sharply diminishing returns.

5. Experiment safely. Saving puts a sentence in the sandbox; your committed collection stays unchanged until you press Commit sandbox. Cancel all changes to restore it. You can edit several sentences, transfer words between them, and see what the portfolio would score.

Limitations: Grammar v0.2 is a research subset of English, so some perfectly good sentences will be rejected as unsupported. Quoted word dumps are not supported. Scores are uncalibrated and intended for playtesting only. This is not connected to your actual Starpin discoveries.
