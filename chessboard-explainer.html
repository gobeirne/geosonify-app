THE IDEA

Not a chess game. A coordinate in disguise.

The 32 visible pieces have standard material and look like a settled chess position. Geosonify turns the board back into its exact original hex string, including leading zeros, without remembering anything about the encoding session.

Both the normal HexByte and HEALPix modes use the same chessboard mathematics. The difference is the coordinate system represented—and the parameter name on the shared board URL.
