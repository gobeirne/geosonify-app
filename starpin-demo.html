the lattice

Coarse orders are drawn boldest because those are the rare ones; hairlines are the commonplace. The grid switches to yellow on aerial, where purple would disappear. Bagged cornerstones are green dots sized by rarity; the crosshair is the one you're heading for. The halo is your real fix accuracy, drawn to scale. A straight line to a point is not a route — use the map to find a way there. Amber dots are catalogue stars whose starpins fall in view; move the map and it looks up what is near the centre.
